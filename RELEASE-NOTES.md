## v1.0.0 — PS5 Controller Bluetooth Supported



Download **Blacklist-DualSense-DropIn.zip**, close Blacklist, and extract into the folder containing Blacklist_Launcher.exe. Launch normally through Ubisoft Connect.



Includes the x86 XInput compatibility DLL, SDL runtime, install/removal instructions and checksums. Adds DualSense identity recognition and prioritizes it ahead of other DirectInput devices, including keyboard game-controller interfaces.



User confirmed controller menus and analog movement with a Bluetooth DualSense in DX11. Automated checks verified mappings, DLL loading and in-game selection. USB, DX9 gameplay, rumble, reconnect and other controller models remain untested. Original game files are not included or modified on disk.



Remove by deleting src/SYSTEM/xinput1_3.dll and src/SYSTEM/SDL3.dll with the game closed; restore any preexisting DLLs from your own backup.
